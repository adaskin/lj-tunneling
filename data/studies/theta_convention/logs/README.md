# logs of the Theta-convention study

The twelve `dynamics_run` logs (N = 2, 3, 4 at k = 12; for each N the four runs of `../theta_variant.py`: the convention `left` at the configured tilt, `half` at the configured tilt, `half` at its own
relocated tilt, `left` at the relocated tilt, in the order of `../theta_variant.log.txt`).  The runs themselves were written to a scratch directory and are not kept; `../summary.csv` and `../theta_variant.json` hold their numbers.
